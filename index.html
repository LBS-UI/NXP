SnapStudio

Camera · Crop · Cloudflare Images

Scan to open the image. Long-press (or right-click → Save image) to save it.

Photos stay on your device until you choose to upload. When you generate a QR code the image is sent directly to Cloudflare Images (via a one-time upload URL obtained from a Cloudflare Worker). The Worker never receives the image bytes. Your Cloudflare API token stays on the server.
